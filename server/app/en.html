Behind every jar, there's a kitchen

Cheffe Suzanne started as a family kitchen. The recipes come from [my mother / my grandmother] and have barely changed: we pick the fruit at the market, cook it, fill the jars and label them by hand.

Today that kitchen is a small workshop and shop in Oran. We sell what we make, plus a few products from growers we know.
